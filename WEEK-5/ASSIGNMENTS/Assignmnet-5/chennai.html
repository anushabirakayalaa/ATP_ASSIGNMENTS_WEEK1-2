Chennai

Chennai, on the Bay of Bengal in eastern India, is the capital of the state of Tamil Nadu. The city is home to Fort St. George, built in 1644 and now a museum showcasing the city’s roots as a British military garrison and East India Company trading outpost, when it was called Madras. Religious sites include Kapaleeshwarar Temple, adorned with carved and painted gods, and St. Mary’s, a 17th-century Anglican church.

Chennai is famous as the "Cultural Capital of South India" for its deep-rooted traditions, vibrant Carnatic music, Bharatanatyam dance, and rich heritage, alongside being a major economic powerhouse known as the "Detroit of India" for its massive auto industry, and a hub for IT, healthcare (medical tourism), and film, all set on the scenic Coromandel Coast. It's a unique blend of ancient culture and modern industry, known for delicious food (filter coffee, dosas), historic temples, and Marina Beach, India's longest urban beach.
